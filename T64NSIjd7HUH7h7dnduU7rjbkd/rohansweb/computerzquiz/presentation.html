02 • Inside the machine

What makes a computer work?

A computer is made from hardware and software. Hardware is the physical equipment you can touch. Software gives the computer instructions.

01
CPU – the processorProcesses instructions and calculations.
02
RAM – working memoryHolds information while programs are running.
03
Storage – keeps your filesStores apps, photos, games and documents.
03 • From idea to computer

How is a computer made?

It takes designers, engineers and software developers working together to create a computer that is ready to use.

1
Someone makes the computerEngineers design and build the physical parts, then put them together.
2
Someone designs the operating systemDevelopers create the OS that manages the computer and runs apps.
3
Someone sets up the computerThe OS and software are installed, tested and prepared for people to use.

06 • Artificial intelligence

What is AI?

Artificial intelligence is technology that can perform tasks that usually need human-like skills, such as recognising pictures, understanding language or finding patterns.

1
AI learns from examplesIt finds patterns in information it has been trained on.
2
AI makes predictionsIt uses those patterns to suggest an answer or action.
3
People stay in chargeAI can make mistakes, so people need to check important results.
Voice assistants
Understand spoken requests.
Image recognition
Identify objects in pictures.
Recommendations
Suggest music or videos.
Creative tools
Help make text, pictures and ideas.
07 • People are in control

We program it.
We control it.

Robots and AI do not simply decide their own purpose. People design them, write their programs and set the rules for what they are allowed to do.

01
We write the codeInstructions tell the robot how to move, sense and respond.
02
We set the rulesPeople choose the task, limits and safety checks.
03
We stay responsiblePeople test the system, check its decisions and can stop it.
AI can suggest an action, but people decide how it should be used. Our code gives the machine its instructions.
