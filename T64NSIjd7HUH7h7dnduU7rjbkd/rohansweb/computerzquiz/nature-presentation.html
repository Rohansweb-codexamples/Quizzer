01 • Living things

What makes something alive?

Living things grow, use energy, respond to their surroundings and reproduce. Plants and animals are both living things, even though they behave in very different ways.

1
They need energyAnimals eat food; green plants use sunlight to make food.
2
They grow and changeA seed can become a tree. A young animal grows into an adult.
3
They respondPlants grow towards light and animals react to danger.
GROWLiving things change as they get older.
NEEDWater, energy and suitable surroundings.
RESPONDThey react to changes around them.
REPRODUCEThey can produce new living things.
02 • Plant power

How do plants make food?

Photosynthesis is the process plants use to make sugar. Their leaves take in carbon dioxide, roots absorb water and sunlight provides the energy.

Sunlight + water + carbon dioxide → sugar + oxygen

Chlorophyll, the green pigment in leaves, helps capture light energy.

LEAVESCollect light and take in carbon dioxide.
ROOTSAbsorb water and minerals from soil.
STEMSSupport the plant and transport water.
FLOWERSHelp many plants reproduce.

05 • Food chains

Energy moves through nature.

A food chain shows how energy passes from one living thing to another. Plants are producers because they make their own food using sunlight.

Sun → grass → rabbit → fox

Animals that eat plants are herbivores. Animals that eat other animals are carnivores. Some eat both and are omnivores.

PRODUCERA plant makes its own food.
HERBIVOREAn animal eats plants.
CARNIVOREAn animal eats other animals.
DECOMPOSERFungi and bacteria break down dead material.
06 • Our planet

Earth is a world of systems.

Earth has layers beneath our feet. The thin outer layer is the crust. Below it are the mantle and the core.

Weather, oceans, rocks and living things interact. Small changes in one part can affect other parts of the planet.

We live on the Earth's crust — the outer rocky layer.
CRUSTThe solid surface where we live.
MANTLEHot rock beneath the crust.
OUTER CORELiquid metal, mostly iron and nickel.
INNER COREA very hot, solid centre.
