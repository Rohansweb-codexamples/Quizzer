What you will learn

  • Identify anthers, stigmas, styles, ovaries and ovules.
  • Explain how insects transfer pollen between flowers.
  • Compare animal and wind pollination.
  • Describe how fertilisation can lead to seeds and fruit.
  • Suggest practical ways to support pollinators without disturbing wildlife.

Try a flower-watching investigation

Choose a flowering plant with an adult. Watch it for five minutes from a safe distance. Record each visitor, how long it stays and which flower parts it touches. Do not catch insects or disturb a nest. Compare your observations on another day.
