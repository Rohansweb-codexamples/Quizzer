Clear the crates

Match beside crates to break them. Make four in a row for a rocket and five for a rainbow ball. Tap a power-up to activate it.

Special tiles: R clears a row and column · D makes a large blast · W clears one tile colour.

Computer: click two neighbouring tiles, or select one and use the arrow keys. Tablet: swipe a tile towards a neighbour. Progress, stars and coins save on this device.
