01 / THE BIG IDEA

Electricity transfers energy.

Electric current is the flow of electric charge around a complete path. A cell or battery supplies energy that can be transferred to components such as lamps, motors and buzzers.

CURRENTMeasured in amperes (A)
VOLTAGEEnergy transferred per unit of charge
ENERGY SOURCEA cell or battery
OUTPUTLight, sound, movement or heat
THE CELL PROVIDES ENERGY TO THE CIRCUIT
02 / COMPLETE THE LOOP

A circuit needs a closed path.

Electric current flows when the conducting path is complete. If there is a gap, such as an open switch or broken wire, the current stops and the lamp goes out.

TRY IT — operate the switch
The switch is closed. The lamp is glowing.
CLOSEDComplete loop — current can flow
OPENGap in the path — current stops
CHANGE THE SWITCH AND WATCH THE LAMP
03 / KNOW THE SYMBOLS

Meet the components.

Circuit diagrams use agreed symbols so that people can understand a circuit without drawing every real object. Wires join the components together.

CELLSupplies electrical energy
LAMPTransfers energy as light and heat
SWITCHOpens or closes the path
BUZZERTransfers energy as sound
CIRCUIT SYMBOLS ARE A SHARED SCIENCE LANGUAGE
04 / MATERIALS

Some materials let charge flow.

Conductors allow electric current to pass through them easily. Metals such as copper are good conductors. Insulators, such as rubber and plastic, resist the flow of current and help keep wires safe.

CONDUCTORCopper, aluminium and many other metals
INSULATORRubber, plastic, glass and dry wood
THE METAL CARRIES CURRENT; THE COATING HELPS PROTECT US
05 / ONE PATH

A series circuit has one route.

In a series circuit, components are connected one after another in a single loop. The same current passes through each component. Adding more lamps in series usually makes each lamp dimmer when the same cell is used.

ONE LOOPThere is only one path for current
BREAK THE LOOPEvery component stops working
A SERIES CIRCUIT HAS A SINGLE CURRENT PATH
06 / CONTROL THE CIRCUIT

A switch makes or breaks a connection.

Inside a simple switch, a metal contact joins two terminals when the switch is closed. Opening it separates the contacts and creates a gap. That is how a switch controls a lamp, buzzer or motor.

CLOSED CONTACTMetal touches metal — path complete
OPEN CONTACTContacts are apart — path broken
THE METAL CONTACT COMPLETES OR BREAKS THE LOOP
07 / SLOWING CURRENT

Resistance opposes current.

Resistance describes how much a component or material opposes electric current. A higher resistance usually means a smaller current for the same voltage. Resistance is measured in ohms (Ω).

UNITOhm (Ω)
MORE RESISTANCELess current at the same voltage
WIRE LENGTHA longer wire usually has more resistance
WIRE THICKNESSA thicker wire usually has less resistance
LENGTH AND THICKNESS AFFECT A WIRE'S RESISTANCE
08 / ENERGY IN ACTION

Electrical energy is transferred.

A battery stores energy chemically. In a circuit, energy is transferred electrically to components. A lamp transfers energy to its surroundings as light and thermal energy.

BATTERYChemical energy store
WIRESElectrical energy transfer
LAMPLight output
SURROUNDINGSThermal energy warms nearby objects
ENERGY IS TRANSFERRED, NOT CREATED BY THE LAMP
09 / BE A SCIENTIST

Plan a fair test.

You can investigate how the number of cells affects a lamp's brightness. Change one factor at a time, keep the other conditions the same and record what you observe.

CHANGENumber of identical cells
MEASURELamp brightness (use the same method)
KEEP THE SAMELamp, wires and test conditions
REPEATTake more than one reading
QUICK CHECK — what should stay the same?
ONLY THE NUMBER OF CELLS CHANGES
10 / STAY SAFE

Electricity needs respect.

Never experiment with mains electricity, wall sockets or exposed wires. Classroom circuit investigations should use low-voltage cells and be checked by an adult.

KEEP DRYKeep electrical equipment away from water
CHECK CABLESNever use damaged plugs or wires
ASK AN ADULTDo not open appliances or wall sockets
USE CELLSUse only the safe low-voltage kit provided
USE LOW-VOLTAGE CLASSROOM KITS AND FOLLOW ADULT GUIDANCE
