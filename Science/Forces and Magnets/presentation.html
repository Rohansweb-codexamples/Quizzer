01 / THE BIG IDEA

A force is a push or pull.

Forces can make something start moving, stop, speed up, slow down, change direction or change shape.

PUSHKicking a football away
PULLOpening a drawer towards you
MEASUREForce is measured in newtons (N)
DIRECTIONForces act in a direction

02 / WHAT HAPPENS?

Balanced or unbalanced?

When forces are balanced, the overall force is zero. When they are unbalanced, the object accelerates in the direction of the larger force.

BALANCEDEqual forces in opposite directions
UNBALANCEDOne force is bigger than the other

03 / EVERYDAY PHYSICS

Friction slows things down.

Friction happens when surfaces rub against each other. It can slow movement, but it is also what helps us walk without slipping.

MORE GRIPRough surfaces usually create more friction
LESS FRICTIONOil and other lubricants help surfaces slide
AIR RESISTANCEAir pushes against moving objects
TRY ITSlide a book across carpet and a table

04 / THE PULL OF EARTH

Meet gravity.

Gravity is an attractive force between masses. Earth's gravity pulls objects towards the centre of our planet.

WEIGHTThe force of gravity on an object
MASSHow much matter an object contains
FALLINGGravity makes dropped objects accelerate
IN SPACEGravity helps keep moons in orbit
↓
EARTH'S GRAVITY PULLS TOWARDS ITS CENTRE
05 / MAGNETIC MATERIALS

Magnets have an invisible pull.

A magnet creates a magnetic field around it. The field can attract some materials without the magnet touching them.

ATTRACTEDIron, steel, nickel and cobalt
NOT MAGNETICWood, plastic and paper
NO TOUCH NEEDEDMagnetic forces can act across a gap
FIELDThe area where magnetic forces act

06 / THE TWO ENDS

North meets south.

Every magnet has two poles. The way the poles face determines whether the magnets pull together or push apart.

NORTH + SOUTHOpposite poles attract
NORTH + NORTHLike poles repel
SOUTH + SOUTHLike poles repel
