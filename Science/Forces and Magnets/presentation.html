Forces & Magnets

Discover pushes, pulls, friction, gravity and the invisible forces made by magnets.

01 · PUSHES AND PULLS

What is a force?

A force is a push or a pull. Forces can start or stop movement, change speed or direction, and change an object's shape.

EXAMPLEA kick pushes a football.
EXAMPLEA hand pulls a drawer.
MEASUREMENTForces are measured in newtons (N).
DIRECTIONA force acts in a particular direction.
02 · FORCE DIAGRAMS

Balanced and unbalanced forces

When forces are balanced, an object's motion does not change. An unbalanced force changes its movement.

BALANCEDEqual forces in opposite directions.
UNBALANCEDOne side has a larger force.
RESULTA net force changes motion.
DIAGRAMSArrows show force size and direction.
03 · SURFACES

Friction

Friction is a force that acts when surfaces rub against each other. It often slows moving objects.

GRIPTyres need friction to grip the road.
ROUGH SURFACESUsually create more friction.
LUBRICANTSReduce friction between surfaces.
DRAGAir resistance is a type of friction.
04 · EARTH'S PULL

Gravity

Gravity is an attractive force between objects with mass. Earth's gravity pulls objects towards its centre.

WEIGHTGravity gives objects their weight.
ON EARTHGravity keeps us on the ground.
IN SPACEGravity helps keep the Moon in orbit.
REMEMBERMass and weight are not the same.
05 · MAGNETIC MATERIALS

Meet magnets

Magnets create a magnetic field around them. Some materials, including iron and steel, are attracted to magnets.

ATTRACTEDIron and many types of steel.
NOT ATTRACTEDWood and plastic.
INVISIBLE FORCEA magnet can pull without touching.
MAGNETIC FIELDThe area where magnetic forces act.
06 · NORTH AND SOUTH

Magnetic poles

Every magnet has a north pole and a south pole. The way poles interact creates attraction or repulsion.

NORTH + SOUTHAttract each other.
NORTH + NORTHRepel each other.
SOUTH + SOUTHRepel each other.
STRONGEST PULLUsually near the poles.
07 · INVISIBLE FIELDS

Magnetic fields

A magnetic field is the region around a magnet where magnetic forces can act. A compass needle turns to line up with a magnetic field.

FIELD LINESShow the direction of the field.
STRENGTHCloser field lines mean a stronger field.
COMPASSIts needle aligns with the field.
EARTHOur planet has its own magnetic field.
